About GrabIt

Built for everyone. Free forever.

What is GrabIt?

GrabIt is a free, browser-based video downloader designed to make saving online videos simple, fast and accessible to everyone. Whether you want to save a YouTube tutorial for offline learning, download an Instagram Reel to share with family, or archive a TED Talk — GrabIt makes it effortless.

Our Mission

We believe in a free and open internet where people can access content on their own terms. GrabIt was built with one mission: to give users a clean, ad-light, privacy-respecting tool that just works — without requiring sign-ups, software installs or technical knowledge.

What We Support

GrabIt supports over 1000 platforms powered by the open-source yt-dlp engine. Our most popular supported platforms include YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Twitter/X, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud and many Indian platforms like ShareChat and Moj. See our full supported sites list.

Privacy First

We do not store your downloads. We do not log the URLs you submit. We do not sell your data. GrabIt collects only minimal, anonymised analytics data to help us improve the service. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Responsible Use

GrabIt is intended for personal, offline use only. We do not support or condone the redistribution, re-uploading or commercial use of downloaded content. Users are responsible for ensuring their use of GrabIt complies with the laws of their country and the terms of service of the source platform.

Contact Us

Have a question, suggestion or found a bug? We'd love to hear from you. Reach out via our Contact page.
