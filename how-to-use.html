Getting Started

Basic Download Guide

01

Find your video

Open YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter or any supported platform. Navigate to the video you want to download.

02

Copy the video URL

On desktop: copy the URL from your browser's address bar. On mobile: tap Share → Copy Link. The URL will look like https://youtube.com/watch?v=...

03

Paste URL into GrabIt

Go to GrabIt's homepage, click the input box and paste the URL. You can use Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac) or long-press to paste on mobile.

04

Click "Grab"

Hit the Grab button. GrabIt will instantly fetch the video information including title, thumbnail and available quality options.

05

Select quality & download

Choose your preferred quality — 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p or Audio Only (MP3). Then click Download. Your file will save to your Downloads folder.

Platform Tips

Platform-Specific Instructions

▶️

YouTube

Copy URL from address bar or click Share → Copy Link. Supports up to 4K and audio-only download. YouTube Shorts also supported.

📸

Instagram

Tap the 3 dots → Copy Link on any Reel, Post or Story. Only public accounts work. Private account videos cannot be downloaded.

📘

Facebook

Click on the video, then copy the URL from your browser. Works for public videos and public group posts only.

🎵

TikTok

Tap Share → Copy Link. GrabIt downloads TikTok videos without the watermark.

🐦

Twitter / X

Copy the tweet URL from your browser. Works for tweets that contain a video. GIF downloads also supported.

🎮

Twitch

Copy the VOD or Clip URL from your browser. Live stream downloading is not supported.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Always use the full URL, not the short title of a video.
  • For Instagram, make sure the account is public.
  • If a download fails, try refreshing the page and pasting the URL again.
  • On iPhone, downloaded videos may open in the browser — tap Share → Save to Files to keep them.
  • Audio-only downloads are great for saving podcasts or music for offline listening.
