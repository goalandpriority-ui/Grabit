General Questions

What is GrabIt? ▾
GrabIt is a free, browser-based video downloader that lets you save videos from over 1000 platforms including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter and many more — directly to your device.
Is GrabIt free to use? ▾
Yes, GrabIt is completely free. There are no hidden charges, no subscriptions and no sign-up required. We sustain the service through non-intrusive advertising.
Do I need to create an account? ▾
No account needed. Just paste a URL and download. We respect your privacy and don't require registration.
How many videos can I download? ▾
There is no download limit. You can download as many videos as you want, as often as you like.
Does GrabIt work on mobile? ▾
Yes! GrabIt works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge — on both Android and iOS devices.
Downloads

Download Questions

What video qualities are available? ▾
Available qualities depend on the source platform. GrabIt typically offers 4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p and Audio Only (MP3). Not all qualities are available for every video.
Can I download audio only (MP3)? ▾
Yes! For supported platforms, GrabIt offers an Audio Only option that extracts the audio track as an MP3 file. This is great for music, podcasts and lectures.
Why is my download failing? ▾
Common reasons: the video is private or restricted, the platform has changed its API, or your internet connection timed out. Try refreshing the page, double-checking the URL and trying again. If the issue persists, contact us.
Where do downloaded files go? ▾
Downloads go to your browser's default download folder. On most devices this is the "Downloads" folder. On iPhone/iPad, you may need to tap "Save to Files" or "Save to Camera Roll" from the share menu.
Can I download private or age-restricted videos? ▾
No. GrabIt can only access publicly available content. Private videos, DRM-protected content and paywalled videos cannot be downloaded.

Legal & Safety

Legal & Privacy

Is it legal to download videos? ▾
Downloading videos for personal, offline viewing is generally acceptable in many countries. However, redistributing, re-uploading or commercially using downloaded content without the creator's permission is illegal. Always respect the original creator's rights. GrabIt is a technical tool — the responsibility for how downloads are used rests with the user.
Does GrabIt store my downloads or browsing history? ▾
No. GrabIt does not store downloaded videos on our servers. We also do not log or save the URLs you enter. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Is GrabIt safe to use? ▾
Yes. GrabIt does not require any browser extensions, software installation or account creation. We do not collect sensitive personal data. Our site is served over HTTPS for secure connections.
Does GrabIt have viruses or malware? ▾
No. GrabIt is a clean web application. We do not distribute software, extensions or executables. If you're ever prompted to install something while using GrabIt, that is not from us.
What does GrabIt do with my data? ▾
We collect minimal, anonymised usage data for analytics purposes (page views, general location). We do not sell your data. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Platform Questions

Can I download YouTube Shorts? ▾
Yes! YouTube Shorts are fully supported. Just copy the Shorts URL and paste it into GrabIt.
Can I download Instagram Reels? ▾
Yes. Instagram Reels, Posts and Stories from public accounts are supported. Private account content cannot be downloaded.
Does GrabIt remove TikTok watermarks? ▾
Yes, GrabIt downloads TikTok videos in their original format without the TikTok watermark.
My platform isn't listed — will it still work? ▾
Possibly! GrabIt supports 1000+ platforms. Just paste the URL and try. If it doesn't work, contact us and we'll look into adding support.
